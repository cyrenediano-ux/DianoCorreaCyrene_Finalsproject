Siram Café is a cozy place where every cup highlights the rich flavors of Bicol. “Siram” means delicious in the Bicol language, and that’s what we serve—coffee that warms the heart and satisfies the soul.

Our menu offers local-inspired blends, from bold brews with pili nut to refreshing tropical cold drinks. With rustic Filipino charm mixed with modern café style, it’s the perfect spot to study, relax, or share stories.
